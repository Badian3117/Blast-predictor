Welcome to the PMP Practice Exam Simulator

This simulator mirrors the computer-based testing experience of the PMP exam (July 2026 Examination Content Outline): 180 questions, 240 minutes, 3 sections of 60 questions with two optional 10-minute breaks. A new random set of questions and answer orders is drawn every time the application is opened.

Tutorial

Exam Tutorial

Take a moment to learn the tools available during the exam. The exam clock does not run during the tutorial.

Previous / NextMove between questions in the current section. Alt+P / Alt+N.
Flag for ReviewMark a question to revisit before ending the section. Alt+F.
Review ScreenSee every question in the section, filter by Incomplete or Flagged, and jump to any question.
HighlightTurn on, then select text in the question to highlight it. Use Clear to remove highlights.
StrikethroughRight-click an answer option (or turn on Strikethrough mode and click it) to cross out options you have eliminated.
CalculatorOn-screen calculator for EVM, PERT and other calculations.
Sections & breaksThe exam is split into sections of 60 questions. Once you end a section you cannot return to it. Between sections you may take an optional 10-minute break; the exam clock is paused during the break.
Question typesMultiple choice (one answer), multiple response (select the number indicated), matching (choose from drop-down lists), fill-in-the-blank (numeric) and graphic/table-based questions.
Time expiryWhen the clock reaches 0:00:00 the exam ends automatically and is scored. Unanswered questions are scored as incorrect — never leave a question blank.
Candidate Agreement

Practice Exam Agreement

To get a realistic measure of your readiness, take this practice exam under real exam conditions:

  • Do not use notes, books, or internet resources.
  • Use only the on-screen calculator and, if you wish, a blank sheet (the real exam provides an on-screen whiteboard).
  • Respect the time limit and take breaks only at the scheduled points.
  • Choose the best answer according to PMI standards (PMBOK® Guide, Agile Practice Guide, Process Groups: A Practice Guide), not necessarily what is done in your organization.
